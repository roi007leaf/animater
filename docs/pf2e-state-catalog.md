# PF2e conditions and persistent effects

Animater includes **43 core conditions** and **2,929 native effect Items**, pinned to PF2e **8.5.1**. This covers 545 spell effects, 721 equipment effects, 853 feat effects, 682 creature effects, 53 other effects, 68 campaign effects, and 7 boon/curse effects. Catalogs are separate from saved recipe slots.

Open **PF2e conditions** or **PF2e effects**. Choose **Use animation** for one entry, or **Use condition/effect catalog** for the whole group. Both work independently of saved-recipe automatic playback. **Pause catalog** ends its existing attached visuals. Per-entry exclusions leave other native states running. Click the entry name to open its native Foundry Item sheet.

**Preview loop** demonstrates six seconds on one affected token, with active-layer highlighting. **Local canvas preview** uses the selected token, creates no condition/effect Item, ends automatically, and leaves existing table visuals alone. Persistent Damage offers all fifteen current damage types, including bludgeoning, piercing, slashing and force. Real playback follows `system.persistent.damageType`. Unknown types use a neutral damage cue rather than assuming bleeding.

**Customize** copies sustained aura layers to Recipes. Their lifecycle remains linked to the native document. Change assets, scale, opacity, colors, filters, position or property tracks; add more attached layers. Save, then choose **Enable customization**. New customizations do not activate a previously disabled catalog. Changing a Persistent Damage type creates a separate customization, so a custom fire loop cannot replace bleed. Manual table playback is blocked for document-linked recipes to prevent orphaned auras. Preview duration is only a sample; live effects have no fixed duration.

## Lifetime and viewers

Prepared active conditions and unexpired effect Items define desired visuals on each visible token. Overrides and lesser duplicate conditions stay quiet. Valued-condition handover reties the visual to its new backing Item. Conditions granted in memory follow their stored granter. Item/actor/token changes, combat updates, world time, viewer changes and canvas readiness reconcile the desired state. Removing a document, becoming inactive, expiring, hiding a token from the current viewer, or pausing its catalog ends only that visual. Reloading or returning to a scene rebuilds still-active states.

Each client creates its own visible Sequencer layers with `.persist().temporary()` and local playback. No saved persistent flags or playback broadcast are needed. Cleanup uses exact client-owned names and `endEffects(filters, false)`; it leaves other users/modules' effects and transient recipe previews intact. Startup hooks cancel visuals removed while media loads. Sequencer 4.2.3 still emits position-update traffic for temporary effect owners; client-local does not mean zero socket traffic. Strict Sequencer creation permissions can prevent a player's local layers and produce Activity diagnostics.

Unidentified effects are GM-only. Secret NPC conditions follow PF2e's metagame setting. Hidden/undetected/unnoticed/invisible and attitude cues are restricted to actor owners and GMs; invisible tokens never gain visible external markers for other viewers. Attached layers bind native visibility, alpha and elevation. No native Item, Token or Actor data is changed by playback. PF2e still applies/removes mechanics; Animater does not infer an effect from a spell's cast when no native effect document exists.

## Art direction and limits

Core conditions have explicit per-condition sustained layouts, interpreted against complete localized native descriptions: **8 themed, 35 symbolic**, with **49 attached layers**. They are no longer all labeled Curated. JB2A lacks literal depictions of many abstract conditions; symbolic treatment does not mean the art exactly depicts the rules. Body cues are centered; face cues have small offsets; Prone uses a flattened ground cue. Installed-edition selection retains the condition layout instead of reapplying a universal overhead marker offset. Saved custom placements remain intact.

**Condition placement:** the location audit covers 43 conditions and 15 damage variants, or **128 layer/edition layouts**, using complete-clip visible-alpha bounds sampled at 12 fps. Off-Guard and Wounded previously shifted the entire baked marker orbit sideways; their offsets now center that orbit on the affected token. The same correction applies to Deafened, Doomed, Friendly, Helpful, Stunned, Stupefied's rune layer, and persistent bleed, bludgeoning and mental damage. Helpful's second heart orbit is mirrored around the same center. Individual symbols still move within these JB2A movies. Concealed uses centered fog instead of three smoke variants with different asymmetric padding. Unconscious uses filename-verified, edition-specific visible-art anchors so its sleep symbols land near the face. New filenames must receive a measured anchor before the builder accepts them. Intentional face and flattened ground offsets remain separate from orbit placement. Final audit: **0 displaced orbits / 0 uncompensated media-center issues**, including every selected video variant. [Location measurements](../data/condition-location-audit.json).

Sickened uses a nausea-colored plume without a poison sigil. Paralyzed uses a stationary outline rather than chains or ice. Drained, Dying and Wounded distinguish depleted life, a pulsing life cue and prior injury; only persistent bleed uses a blood-drop cue. Unconscious uses sleeping symbols. Attitude visuals are private cues, not healing or buffs. Skull-mask footage is excluded from condition veils. Persistent sonic, vitality and void cues no longer use musical notes, healing hearts or cold footage.

Localized descriptions resolve against pinned native English text. Reviewed potion protection, reuse cooldowns, flight, and Fire/Glass/Prismatic Shield effects receive explicit treatments. Ordinary statistical buffs still use themed or symbolic bearer overlays; this is not a claim of 2,929 bespoke depictions. Mirror Image uses a symbolic shimmer rather than mechanically accurate image-count copies. Nine native effect documents have no description text; their symbolic treatment uses names/rules and this omission is recorded in coverage data.

**Native aura fields:** 114 emitter/recipient entries now have 88 distinct two-layer compositions, reviewed against full effect descriptions and linked spell, feat or equipment providers. The 59 emitting effect documents and linked equipment/feat sources use the actor's final prepared aura radius, including native growth and merged kinetic-aura radius. Recipient copies keep token-sized overlays. Protective Wards uses glyphs and a refractive boundary; Protector's Sphere uses a shield shell and energy lining; Shattershields uses orbiting metal; Tempest Cloak uses wind. Air/Earth forms no longer inherit fire imagery from another Element Embodied form. Life Burn uses force rather than flames. Corresponding emitter/recipient documents for the same ability intentionally share a composition.

**Preview aura choice** offers Thermal Nimbus fire/cold and Manifest Will traditions. Live fields read the native prepared selection; an unknown choice uses an explicitly neutral field. Every layer shares the source document's lifetime and cleanup namespace. Merged aura effect parents identify actual contributing stance sources, so a generic Kinetic Aura no longer masks the stance.
